# Safety requirements

## Universal limits

- Facilitate at least one adult per active build table. Pilot each configuration before the event.
- Use hand power or battery-powered devices at **5 V or less**. No wall power, combustion, compressed-air vessels, needles, or cutting of metal by students.
- Require eye protection for any drilling, cutting, or test involving a suspended load. Facilitators—not students—operate drills, saws, hot glue, or laser cutters.
- Keep fingers clear of gears, winch drums, and linkage pivots while a mechanism is moving. Tie back hair; do not wear loose sleeves or jewelry.
- Inspect all fasteners, sharp edges, tubing connections, and load attachments between groups. Remove damaged components from service.

## Project-specific boundaries

| Project | Required boundary |
| --- | --- |
| HydroGrab | Water only; use needleless syringes. Wipe spills immediately and protect nearby electronics. Lift foam, paper, or other light objects only. |
| Rescue Hoist | Establish a 250 g maximum suspended mass and a 20 cm lift height. Use a soft catcher below the payload. Do not hold a hand or foot under the load. |
| Crank Critter | Use rounded craft sticks and blunt fasteners. Stop the mechanism before adjusting pivots. |
| Pinball Lab | Use only one 3/8–1/2 in ball per table and a raised perimeter. Do not aim or release the ball toward people. |
| Cable-Car Dash | Counterweights must remain below 250 g and within a clear vertical drop zone. Keep hands away from rope and pulleys while moving. |
| Gear Garden | Cover or space exposed gear meshes so fingers cannot enter while cranking. |
| Springy Wobblebot | Use a 3 V battery supply only; disconnect before adjusting the eccentric mass. Do not use damaged cells or a swollen battery. |

## Stop-work conditions

Stop the activity and correct the setup if a frame cracks, a gear skips repeatedly, a rope frays, a fastener loosens, water leaks near electrical equipment, or the required force feels excessive. These are observations of a design limitation, not a reason to force the mechanism to continue.
